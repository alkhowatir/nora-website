Service

Project Supervision & Site Execution.

Full monitoring and control of the project: analysing scope, available resources, and timing, with detailed reporting to protect quality and fidelity to the design.

Service · Supervision & execution

Guarding architectural fidelity from ground-break to handover.

Even the most brilliant architectural vision can unravel if site execution lacks rigorous oversight. Our supervision team acts as the client’s dedicated technical guardian on site, ensuring that every detail on the drawings is translated into construction with uncompromised precision.

We conduct structured site inspections, verify physical material mock-ups against approved specifications, monitor contractor milestones, and resolve unexpected site conditions proactively. Transparent reports keep clients fully informed without the burden of day-to-day site disputes.

Site Supervision & Quality Audit Specification NAS-SUP-01
Deliverables

Key deliverables & scope

Concrete design packages and technical documentation delivered with precision.

01

Quality Control & Site Audits

Regular on-site inspections verifying structural integrity, material fidelity, and craftsmanship standards.

02

Material Mock-up Approvals

Physical sample testing and sign-off on concrete, stone, timber, glazing, and MEP installations.

03

Schedule & Resource Tracking

Continuous tracking of contractor milestones, progress timelines, and milestone verification.

04

Client Reporting & Handover

Detailed photographic progress reports, snagging lists (punch lists), and final building handover audit.

Methodology

How we deliver this discipline

A disciplined, four-stage progression from brief to completion.

  1. 01

    Pre-Construction Plan Review

    Cross-referencing all architectural, structural, and MEP sets prior to ground-breaking.

  2. 02

    Milestone Site Inspections

    Verifying key phases: foundations, concrete pours, waterproofing, rough-ins, and finishes.

  3. 03

    Quality & Material Verification

    Testing on-site mock-ups, reviewing contractor submittals, and enforcing tolerances.

  4. 04

    Snagging & Project Handover

    Documenting deficiency punch-lists, supervising rectification, and executing final handover.
