Service

Landscape Architecture & Outdoor Living.

Landscape and outdoor seating design that builds harmony between people and place, reinforcing the strong relationship between garden environments and architectural mass.

Service · Landscape

Extending architecture into outdoor living sanctuaries.

In the climate of the Arabian Gulf, outdoor spaces must be sculpted with intentionality. We treat the garden not as a decorative perimeter, but as an outdoor architectural room—an extension of interior living spaces that moderates microclimates, provides acoustic calm, and frames views.

Our landscape design balances hardscape elements (paving, pergolas, retaining walls, swimming pools) with softscape selections tailored to regional heat and water efficiency. Every pathway, water feature, and lighting accent is carefully positioned to invite lingering.

Methodology

How we deliver this discipline

A disciplined, four-stage progression from brief to completion.

  1. 01

    Microclimate & Soil Study

    Assessing solar path, prevailing winds, soil conditions, and site privacy requirements.

  2. 02

    Outdoor Spatial Concept

    Mapping outdoor zones: dining, lounging, water elements, and transitional green corridors.

  3. 03

    Hardscape & Planting Details

    Selecting natural pavers, designing custom shade structures, and engineering plant layouts.

  4. 04

    Irrigation & Lighting Integration

    Coordinating smart irrigation systems, drainage infrastructure, and landscape lighting circuits.
