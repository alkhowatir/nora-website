The office

An engineering office with considerable experience and a lot of passion.

We provide engineering and design services at a high level of professionalism and reliability. We listen to our clients’ vision and concepts and transform them into area, space and mass.

Our vision

Create a renewed, creative architectural environment that reflects on society and affects it positively.

What we do

Six disciplines, from first idea to site.

Architecture, interiors, furniture, identity, landscape and project delivery, all shaped by one design line.

01

Architecture

Architectural design for every type of project (residential, commercial and other uses), shaped around the client’s needs and requirements and our own design line.

02

Interiors

Interior design for residential, commercial and other projects: from schematic design and 3D visuals to materials selection, balancing function with beauty.

03

Furniture design

Turning imagination and inspiration into pieces that follow our design line, fit the space and meet the client’s needs.

04

Identity & branding

Creating and developing logos, packaging and product design that support the growth of the client’s project and sit naturally within its space.

05

Landscape

Landscape and outdoor seating design that builds harmony between people and place, reinforcing the strong relationship between landscape and architectural mass.

06

Supervision & execution

Full monitoring and control of the project: analysing scope, available resources and timing, with a complete and detailed report to the client to protect quality and fidelity to the design.

How we work

From listening to delivery.

    Listen

  3. 03
